How cookies work on this website

{companyName} may use essential website storage and, where enabled, limited analytics technologies to support website functionality and understand usage.

Overview

Cookies are small pieces of data that can be stored in a browser when a visitor uses a website.

This website uses a compact cookie preference card that allows visitors to record whether they accept or decline optional cookie use.

Preference control

Your choice is stored locally in the browser so the website can remember that preference.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies and similar browser storage mechanisms can help websites remember settings, maintain functionality and, where applicable, understand general visitor behaviour.

Some cookies are necessary for website operation, while others may be optional depending on the tools used.

Cookie Categories

The exact technologies used may depend on the website configuration. The main categories are described below.

Essential

Required

Supports basic website operation and preference storage required for functionality.

Analytics

Optional

May help understand general website usage where analytics tools are enabled.

Preferences

Functional

Remembers choices such as whether cookie use has been accepted or declined.

Third Party

Depends on tools

May be created by independent services if third-party features are introduced.

Essential Cookies

Essential browser storage may be used to support website functionality that does not reasonably work without it.

  • Remembering cookie consent choices.
  • Supporting basic security or website functionality.
  • Maintaining necessary technical state.
Necessary functionality

Essential technologies are generally used only where required for the website to function as intended.

Analytics Cookies

Limited analytics may be used to understand how visitors interact with the website, where such analytics are enabled.

  • General page usage.
  • Traffic sources.
  • Device or browser categories.
  • General interaction patterns.
  • Website performance trends.

Analytics data should be used to improve website experience, content and technical performance.

Preference Storage

The cookie consent component included on this website stores your selection in browser local storage using a website-specific key.

This allows the website to avoid repeatedly displaying the preference card after you have already made a choice.

Browser-based choice

Clearing browser storage may remove your saved preference, causing the cookie card to appear again.

Third-Party Cookies

If third-party tools, embeds or analytics services are used, those providers may set or access cookies according to their own policies.

{companyName} does not control independent third-party cookie practices.

  • Analytics providers.
  • Embedded media providers.
  • External marketing platforms.
  • Website infrastructure providers.

Managing Cookies

Visitors can manage cookies through the website preference card and through browser settings.

  1. Use the website cookie card to accept or decline optional cookie use.
  2. Use browser privacy settings to block or delete cookies.
  3. Clear browser local storage if you want the website to ask for your preference again.
  4. Review third-party privacy settings for external services where relevant.

Blocking certain cookies or browser storage may affect the behaviour of some website features.

Cookie Retention

Cookie and browser storage duration depends on the purpose of the technology being used.

Some storage may exist only for a browser session, while preference choices may remain until they are manually removed or replaced.

This Cookie Policy may be updated if website technologies, analytics tools or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
